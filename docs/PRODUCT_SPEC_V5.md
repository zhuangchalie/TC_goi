# GuardianAI V5 — Product spec

### Target
Older adults who should not need to understand technology.

### One-time caregiver setup
- contacts
- risk threshold
- permissions/roles
- optional AI server

### Zero-touch daily experience
No upload. No transcript button. No "analyze" button.

### Protection layers
1. Caller screening.
2. SMS screening.
3. Accessible-recording monitor.
4. Whisper/OpenVINO transcription.
5. Explainable scam scoring.
6. Family escalation.
7. Anti-spam cooldown.
8. Local-first fallback.

### Senior UX
- 3 states only: Safe / Caution / Scam.
- large typography
- voice-friendly wording
- one emergency button
- no technical jargon
- alert persists until dismissed by a simple "Tôi hiểu" button.

### Safety
- no covert recording
- no raw audio sent to family by default
- clear consent for recording analysis
- do not claim audio analysis when recording is unavailable
